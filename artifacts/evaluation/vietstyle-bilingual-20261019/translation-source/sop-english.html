1. Purpose and Scope

Applies to fashion and outdoor SKUs in warehouses / fulfillment locations in the VietStyle Commerce model. This SOP covers cycle counts, event-triggered counts, and variance adjustments. It does not replace receiving procedures or QA for returned goods.

2. When Is an Ad Hoc Count Required?

  • A SKU has a fill rate below 95% or experiences a stockout while the system still records high inventory.
  • Both stockout_flag and overstock_flag are enabled; recalculated days of supply exceeds 120 days while a stockout signal remains.
  • A SKU has recurring variances, high value, suspected loss, barcode errors, or size/color mismatches.
  • After a major campaign, warehouse-location change, SKU-code change, large return, or system incident.

3. Preparation Before Counting

  • The Inventory Controller exports the SKU list, location, unit of measure, snapshot timestamp, and system quantity.
  • The Warehouse Supervisor assigns counters; counters must not see system quantities when blind counting is used.
  • Freeze transactions at the location being counted or record every transaction occurring during the count window.
  • Prepare scanners, quarantine labels, recording forms, cameras, and a plan for goods being picked, packed, or transferred.

4. Counting and Reconciliation

  • Count by SKU + size + color + location; do not combine similar variants.
  • Record actual quantity, unit of measure, counter, timestamp, and packaging condition.
  • If a variance exists, have a second person recount before changing the data.
  • Reconcile receipts, picks, shipments, returns, transfers, cancellations, and transactions pending synchronization.
  • Absolute variance = |counted quantity - system quantity|; variance rate = variance / max(system quantity, 1).

6. Do Not Automatically Reorder When Data Conflicts

If recalculated inventory indicates more than 120 days of supply but a stockout signal exists (stockout_days >= 1 or fill_rate < 95%), mark DATA CONFLICT. Temporarily block automatic PO recommendations and check available inventory, held stock, unshipped orders, the snapshot timestamp, and the days-of-supply formula. Reopen the replenishment decision only after the Inventory Controller records a conclusion.

7. System Update and Ticket Closure

  • Every adjustment must include a ticket ID, policy ID, SKU, before/after quantities, reason code, evidence, data-entry person, and approver.
  • Do not delete old transactions or directly edit source files; use traceable adjustment transactions.
  • Notify the Planner when a variance affects ROP, target stock, or order fulfillment capability.
  • Close the ticket after approval, successful system synchronization, and exception-report updates.

8. Records Retention

Retain count lists, recount results, photos / supporting documents, root-cause analyses, approvals, transaction IDs, and synchronization timestamps for the retention period specified by the business. For AI/RAG data, retain metadata: policy_id, owner, effective_date, version, approval_status, and source_document.
