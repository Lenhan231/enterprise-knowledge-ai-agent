`ok` = retain; `fix` = repair the indicated fields before locking; `replace` = replace the repeated target with the exact proposed unused target. Evidence numbers mean **TRANG CHÍNH SÁCH**, and PL-A/B/C mean the named appendices. Each row checks support/completeness, every existing required substring group, overlap, and answerability. Quotes are verbatim from the supplied local policy, not PDF page numbers. “No overlap” means no repeated **target fact** found; related supporting facts and history reuse are called out separately. New approval/expense inputs are allowed by the task.

**Scoring convention:** the proposed `must_include` arrays below are exact replacement text, using `a|b` as alternatives. They remain lexical screening aids, not semantic proofs: a correct Vietnamese paraphrase can avoid any finite phrase list, and a wrong negated answer can mention every keyword. For every case, accept equivalent Vietnamese expressions and verify the required relationships, negation, qualifications, and completeness against the reference. Numeric formatting alternatives are included where relevant; semantic review must reject a wrong amount that merely embeds a matching substring. Empty arrays in abstention/ambiguity/RBAC cases require a behavioral check; they are not an automatic pass. Unmentioned fields stay as drafted.
